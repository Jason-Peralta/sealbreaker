# 0023 — Mobs carry our attributes and can use our weapons at reduced value
Date: 2026-09-06
Status: accepted
Context: our attributes were going to sit on players only, on the assumption that our weapons are player equipment. But a zombie that spawns with, or picks up, one of our swords should be able to use it: that is a fight the player remembers, and a reason to care where loot ends up. Giving a mob a player-tuned weapon at full value would be lethal at Tier 1, and giving it a different weapon entirely would mean two of everything.
Decision: every living entity carries our attributes. A mob keeps the weapon's moveset, reach and effects in full, and its numbers (damage, healing, knockback) are scaled by `sb_core:weapon_proficiency`: 1.0 for players, 0.5 for everything else, times the `difficulty.mob_weapon_proficiency_scalar` config value. A designer raises a specific entity type's base value so an elite or a boss handles gear properly; a server owner turns the scalar down to nothing to disable stolen weapons entirely.
Consequences: one weapon serves both sides, so a special attack authored once shows up in enemy hands with no extra content; balance for mobs is two numbers rather than a parallel item set. The swing system currently accepts only players, so generalising it to any `LivingEntity` is combat-track work before this can be seen in game; the attribute, the scalar and the multiplication are in place so that change is a type change rather than a redesign. Mob attribute instances cost a little memory per entity, which is why the list stays short.
