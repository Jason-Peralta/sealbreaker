# 0016 — Outcomes of the reconciliation with the parallel research
Date: 2026-09-05
Status: accepted (refines 0001, 0008, 0011)
Context: a parallel research and PRD pass ("Astra") recorded answers that conflicted with this project's decisions; `docs/04-reconciliation.md` listed them as C1–C8. The user settled them on 5 Sep 2026.
Decision:
- C1–C3: this project's design stands. Seals are world-wide; gifted gear is usable by anyone; bosses gate our own material tiers, realms and NPCs, and vanilla iron, gold and diamond stay ungated.
- C4: encounters and economy are tuned for 1–4 players; **v1.0 has a capacity ceiling of 10 concurrent players**, load-tested (a mixed 10-player workload and a 10-player boss roster must run without crashes or exclusions; balance is promised only for 1–4).
- C5: the module split is "as many modules as needed": no fixed count, no forced collapse; a module is created when it has a clear owner and boundary and merged when the boundary stops paying for itself.
- C6: Minecraft 26.2 stays. 26.1.2 offers nothing 26.2 lacks, 26.2 is already built and verified, and 26.3 arrives this month.
- C7–C8: this project's hardening model and slice budget stand.
Consequences: the PRD's players/performance NFRs read 10 instead of 20; the eight-module plan is a starting point, not a rule; the end-of-M1 module checkpoint becomes an ordinary review item rather than a decision gate.
