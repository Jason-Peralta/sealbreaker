# 0007 — Movement and defence are earned through accessories; combining is a major system
Date: 2026-09-04
Status: accepted
Context: the user wants Terraria's structure where you start with no movement options and pick up accessories that let you move and dodge better, and a workshop-style combining system that is "a big part of it".
Decision: at spawn the player has vanilla movement and vanilla shield blocking only. Kit accessories introduce dash (Tier 1, guaranteed in Boss I's loot bag), dodge roll (Tier 2), double jump (Tier 3), glide and wall-jump (Tier 4), air dash (Tier 5); each boss is designed around the kit of its tier. Parry is deferred; the hook stays in the combat core. Accessory combining (two accessories plus a catalyst become one that keeps both effects) is a core system from the slice onward, with tree depth varying per accessory and at least one super accessory that needs nearly everything, planned later. The only new keybind is Dash/Dodge.
Consequences: kit accessories grant capability flags read by the combat core; only one capability of each kind is active at a time; Curios provides slots; later-tier kit pieces are sketches until their design session.
