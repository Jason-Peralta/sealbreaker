# 0012 — Settings, bosses, unlocks and names are placeholders until a design session fills them
Date: 2026-09-04
Status: accepted
Context: the user wants the tier structure kept but the concrete settings, boss names and unlocks left open.
Decision: the seven-tier skeleton and its rules are fixed; settings, bosses, unlocks, realm themes, NPC names, currency, materials and block-family names are [...] placeholders. Tier 1 is filled in a design session before Milestone 1 art starts; later tiers are filled tier by tier after the MVP. docs/02-prd.md Appendix A is non-binding scratch.
Consequences: sessions must not invent lore or later-tier content in passing; the working title Sealbreaker and the word Seal are placeholders too.
