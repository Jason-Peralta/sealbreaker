# 0008 — World-wide Seals, per-player boss bags with permanent upgrades
Date: 2026-09-04
Status: accepted
Context: Terraria's model: bosses progress the world for everyone, while each player earns their own upgrades and re-fights bosses to collect them.
Decision: Seals (boss kills) are world-wide; late joiners find the world already progressed. Every participant receives a personal loot bag holding the boss's permanent upgrade (an accessory slot for bosses I–V; the final boss's capstone perk is decided later), the tier's kit accessory and rolled loot; a second bag from the same boss gives loot only. Heart shards found in the world raise max health up to four times base by the endgame; mana crystals raise mana; bosses are tuned to a per-tier health/defence budget. Bosses can always be summoned again.
Consequences: a CharacterProgress attachment per player; the Curios slot count reads it; a compact health display option for 40 hearts; catch-up materials sold by the guide NPC at a premium.
